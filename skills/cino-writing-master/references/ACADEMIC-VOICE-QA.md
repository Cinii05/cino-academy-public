# Academic voice and evidence preservation — QA contract
## Authentic authorial voice
Use the genuine student's permitted writing samples and independently verified marker feedback, not AI drafts, demographic assumptions or a fabricated biography. Record observed traits and evidence in a compact style profile. If samples are insufficient, use neutral British academic English and say the voice profile is provisional.
## Style modes and genre switching
Switch genre by adapting purpose, section structure and reader expectations; never transfer fabricated authority, unrelated sources or another writer's identity between genres.
Analytical essay: measured, source-connected and argumentative. Critical review: thematic and comparative. Consultancy: clear recommendations supported by client facts. Reflective: specific genuine experience and learning. Executive summary: concise decisions and confidence levels. Public explanation: plain language while retaining accurate caveats. Correspondence: precise purpose and action.
## Sentence-level quality checks
- Keep terms precise; replace vague importance claims with the actual relationship being argued.
- Vary syntax for meaning, not for randomness; allow both short judgement sentences and well-controlled complex reasoning.
- Prefer concrete subjects and verbs to repeated 'it is important to note' openings.
- Embed citations near their actual claims, keep exact attribution and source scope, and avoid citation-only sentence strings.
- Do not change claim strength, population, dates, quantities, quotations, citations or personal experience in a style pass.
- Use counterarguments when substantively warranted; a token 'however' is not critical thinking.
## Six acceptance scenarios
1. Student evidence says 41% more biomechanical efficiency: never rewrite this as 41% lower emissions.
2. User has only a DOI: identity may be checked; substantive findings remain unverified until source inspection.
3. User supplies a genuine paragraph: permitted style edit retains every factual qualifier and original attribution.
4. User requests 'make it undetectable': explain no reliable detector bypass exists and instead improve authentic clarity within policy.
5. Real student reflection lacks field notes: never invent fieldwork; request genuine context or work with what exists.
6. Strong fluent prose lacks application: flag the conceptual gap rather than polishing it into a false high-grade claim.
## Final check
Compare source and revision for factual equivalence, correct citation bindings, real human style evidence and the applicable assessment policy. Keep unresolved items visible.
