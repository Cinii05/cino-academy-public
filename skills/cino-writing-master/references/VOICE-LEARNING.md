# Academic voice — author-owned learning from accepted edits

This is an optional addition to Cino Academy's `ACADEMIC-TWIN.md` and `ACADEMIC-VOICE-QA.md`. No actual user corpus belongs in the plugin ZIP. Voice is editorial guidance, not authorship proof, detector evasion or permission to draft assessed coursework.

## Evidence and consent

- For each sample, record local/document locator, genre, source authorship status `SELF_AUTHORED_VERIFIED|AI_DRAFT|OTHER|UNKNOWN`, explicit author consent, assessment privacy and the actual segment inspected. Only author-confirmed SELF_AUTHORED samples inform Academic Twin's personal voice traits.
- An AI draft edited by a student does not become fully SELF_AUTHORED by appearance. **Only the student's actual identified changes** may inform preferences after consent.
- Keep separate profiles for analytic essays, consultancy reports, personal reflections and non-assessed messages. Do not infer personal history, personality, background or emotion from prose.
- A trait entry records the two contrasting examples, genre, evidence provenance, user acceptance, confidence as observed sample coverage (not a quantified identity claim) and an expiry/review option.
- Never reuse old assignment claims, sources, numbers, private client facts, quoted passages or fabricated first-person events. If examples cannot be safely obtained, choose NO_PROFILE and edit neutrally.

## Learning loop

1. Establish policy: assessed task may ban editing or new drafts. Respect it before studying or applying samples.
2. Collect at least two distinct genuine samples if available; reserve another for **held-out style comparison**. Sparse/no-profile modes must work when these are unavailable.
3. Extract observable style traits: noun/verb choice, sentence rhythm, legitimate hedging, paragraph shape, typical transitions, directness and discipline-specific terminology.
4. Show the author a brief proposed style sheet with one positive and one counterexample for each meaningful preference. Ask them to accept, reject or alter the proposed traits.
5. After a permitted draft edit, compare original and user-adjusted spans. Infer **candidate** edit rules only from precise user-made changes and require acceptance before adding them to the voice profile.
6. Evaluate on held-out genuine samples for acceptable fit, with the author's judgement decisive. A style metric is diagnostic only; no claim of biometric verification or detector bypass.
7. Apply fidelity locks before a rewrite: exact quotations, author names/citations, values and units, reasoning stance, causal/association distinction, hedges, methodology, real first-person experience and material counterarguments.
8. If any lock fails, revert the affected edit or send the proposed substantive change back to Research/Evidence. Limit repetitions; never hide a mismatch behind smoother wording.

## Reader check

Offer `FAITHFUL` (minimal), `CLEARER` (logical exposition) or `CONCISE` (cuts repetition) when that helps. The author chooses among alternatives; do not force rigid lists of banned words or punctuational mannerisms. Preserve legitimate terminology, context and precision.
