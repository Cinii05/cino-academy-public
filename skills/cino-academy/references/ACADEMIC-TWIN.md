# Academic Twin operating rules

Academic Twin is an optional style-preservation layer, not an authorship simulator and not a source of facts.

## Profile modes
- `NO_PROFILE`: no valid self-authored evidence. Use neutral academic voice.
- `SPARSE`: limited valid self-authored evidence. Apply only directly evidenced style traits and keep intervention small.
- `RICH`: multiple suitable self-authored samples support a more stable style profile.

Profile mode is descriptive, not a quality score.

## Allowed evidence
Use genuine user-authored writing and genuine marker feedback about writing style. Keep assignment authority, academic sources, primary evidence and prior factual content separate from style evidence.

Previous coursework may evidence sentence length, register, transition habits, paragraph rhythm, hedging patterns and similar style features. It must not silently donate:
- claims or conclusions;
- citations or references;
- statistics or factual assertions;
- personal biographical claims;
- client/project facts;
- assignment-specific wording;
- known errors.

## Preservation
Voice transformation must preserve substantive meaning, qualification boundaries, citation bindings, numerical facts, claim strength and paragraph purpose. If a style change would alter any of those, do not apply it.

Do not add deliberate mistakes, fake quirks, slang, filler, detector-evasion edits, invented preferences, demographic traits, personality claims or unsupported personal history.

## Conflict handling
Current assessment requirements outrank style preference. If a profile trait conflicts with required academic form, accessibility, citation rules or the user's explicit current instruction, preserve the requirement and record the suppressed trait when material.

Academic Twin never upgrades evidence status and never turns GENERATED_OUTPUT into SELF_AUTHORED evidence merely because the output resembles the user's style.

## Context-sensitive editorial calibration (candidate)

When real user-authored examples and valid permission exist, extract only observable traits such as sentence rhythm, hedging discipline, level of formality, use of transitions and paragraph organisation. Record evidence for each adopted trait. Do not infer age, background, personality or supposed emotions from style. Do not use generated drafts as new SELF_AUTHORED training samples.

Treat generic anti-AI word bans and punctuation bans as potentially harmful: academics legitimately use specialised language, citations, complex qualification and diverse punctuation. Preserve the user's evidenced voice while keeping disciplinary and assessment requirements first. A changed claim, citation, statistic, caveat, level of certainty, or invented personal example is a failed rewrite.

Before applying a voice transformation, record the current assessment GenAI permissions; if unknown or restrictive, restrict use to permitted feedback on the student's own work. When a drafting workflow is permitted, compare original and revised passages for factual and citation equivalence. For subjective naturalness, request user feedback rather than claiming authorship authentication or AI-detector evasion.

## Integration with Academic Writing Master (candidate)

For the actual editing of supplied text, Academic Writing Master may use a valid Academic Twin profile strictly for observable style traits. The master must never promote generated drafts into SELF_AUTHORED samples, fill unknown biography, change claim strength, or override a current assessment's AI-use rule. When revising in the absence of a genuine profile, use neutral discipline-appropriate writing and avoid claiming to imitate the student's unique voice.
