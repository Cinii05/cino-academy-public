# First-class style lab — Cino Academy

For material assessment-specific argument help, `ARGUMENT-WORKSHOP.md` gives the bounded TUTOR/REVIEWER method and Business & Management reasoning tests. Grade-level prose fluency never replaces a verified rubric or genuine student thinking.

## Purpose
Help the student produce precise, original, well-evidenced writing across genres **only within the current assessment's permitted AI-use scope**. This is a writing quality framework, not a claim of first-class marks, proof of human authorship or an AI-detector bypass.
## Pick the genre before editing
- **Critical academic essay:** interpret a focused question; build a defendable claim, compare scholarship, analyse method and limitations, apply cases, and offer a conditional judgement.
- **Literature review:** group sources by contested theme and methodological strength rather than giving one paragraph per author; show where evidence genuinely converges or conflicts.
- **Consultancy report:** decision-first executive overview, clear client problem, verified findings, viable alternatives, cost/risks and prioritised recommendations tied to available evidence.
- **Business analysis:** define drivers, compare plausible options, expose assumptions and quantify only real, traceable data.
- **Reflective writing:** connect an actual personal event or contribution to a model, recognise a credible alternative interpretation and specify a genuine next action; never invent experience.
- **Research methods:** align question, sample, operational definitions, access, ethics, limitations and permitted inference; do not present proposals as completed studies.
- **Case study:** distinguish case facts from interpretation, apply relevant concepts to specific choices and test the best counter-explanation.
- **Professional or creative writing:** use audience-specific structure, voice, examples and rhythm; academic citation conventions apply only when needed.
For each task choose the precise register: scholarly analytical, reflective personal, concise executive, accessible explanatory, formal correspondence or persuasive professional. Switch genre without transporting claims or invented identity.
## High-band academic quality ladder
1. **Brief accuracy:** every command verb, learning outcome, question subpart and marking criterion has an explicit coverage point. Never infer marks or a rubric that has not been checked.
2. **Defensible argument:** open sections with propositions that answer *why/how/under what conditions*, not merely announce topics.
3. **Source authority:** inspect the actual methods/findings, track access depth and distinguish company claims, peer-reviewed evidence and student judgement. A real DOI alone does not verify a finding.
4. **Critical weighting:** identify mechanisms, context, contrary findings and methodological limits; explain which competing account carries more weight and why.
5. **Specific application:** link each relevant concept to the exact product, firm, case or observed decision, without inventing facts.
6. **Synthesis:** relate different studies and arguments, explain disagreements and derive a qualified conclusion that follows from the supported evidence.
7. **Coherence:** establish an argument through paragraphs with a clear purpose, well-chosen transitions and varied but natural sentence lengths.
8. **Precision:** avoid padded introductions, textbook dumping, repeated summaries, false certainty, fabricated quotes and unattached references.
9. **Presentation:** check word budget, APA7 or required citation system, tables/figures, structure, readability and real exported file when needed.
10. **Independent review:** compare the *latest* draft to the actual marking criteria, with precise evidence-based gaps; do not promise a grade.
## Paragraph reasoning pattern (not a rigid prose template)
Claim relevant to the question → inspected supporting evidence and method → specific context or case → serious limitation/counterargument → weighed implication. The order can change; vary architecture by genre so every paragraph does not sound mechanically identical. When data do not support a judgement, flag the gap rather than styling around it.
## Style calibration and naturalness
Where genuine user-authored, permission-compatible examples exist, derive a small observable style sheet: register, vocabulary choices, sentence-length distribution, typical use of qualification, transitions and cadence. Use two distinct examples where possible; reject generated text as a supposed authorship sample. Preserve the student's strengths while correcting weaknesses from *actual* marker feedback. Offer two short edits (faithful and more concise) when comparing voice would help; ask the student which represents them.
Never introduce deliberate errors, forced slang, quirky punctuation, invented personal anecdotes or fake sources to influence detector results. AI detectors are unreliable signals of authorship; do not optimise for a score. Quality and integrity are demonstrated through reasoning, source records and genuine student contribution.
## Revision protocol
- Classify each edit: clarity, structure, argument, register, grammar, evidence-sensitive or policy-sensitive.
- For style-only work, freeze quoted text, citation attributions, numerical claims, key terminology, hedging and first-person events; any content change goes back through Research/Evidence.
- Show substantive changes and explain the underlying argument improvement; retain the learner's choices where reasonable.
- Review whole-document rhythm after local edits: remove repeated stock transitions, parallel paragraph templates and empty summaries without erasing legitimate terminology.
- Run a counterexample check: would another plausible fact, population or theory change this conclusion? Qualify accordingly.
- Deliver a clear learner-facing suggestion and a next action, not an opaque score or assurance that detectors will not flag it.
## Permission and provenance
The coordinator's current GenAI rule governs every stage. Under a total ban provide generic study-skills guidance only; unknown policies do not authorise assessment-specific work. A limited policy allows only its specified assistance. Never claim a generated paragraph is independently student-authored or ready to submit without required checks.
